# BENNETTAI074 — Intertidal Harvest Planner (Concept-Stage)

**Bennett Coastal Harvest Intelligence · Bennett AI Solutions Inc.**

BENNETTAI074 is a concept-stage planning tool for licensed gooseneck-barnacle (percebes) harvesters and co-managed fisheries. It combines tide timing, site conditions, and permit rules into a structured go/no-go harvest brief — with a public, no-hidden-weights rule engine and a harvest limiter.

**It plans and screens trips. It does not harvest.** The autonomous drone harvester described in program materials is a long-horizon research target, not a current capability.

## Status

Concept-stage. No customers, revenue, deployments, pilots underway, patents, or validated outcomes claimed. All numeric rules are sample values for inspection — binding limits come from regional regulators and must be versioned and verified per season.

## Program framing

- **Problem:** dangerous, regulated, high-value fishery; trip planning is informal and dead trips are common.
- **What exists:** the rules engine, sample rule sets, methodology, and this demo.
- **What is being tested:** whether a structured pre-trip brief reduces dead trips and permit incidents without reducing legal harvest.
- **Pilot ask:** 1–2 licensed crews / one cofradía (Galicia) or one co-managed fishery partner (BC) for one harvest window.
- **Commercial structure:** design partnership, sponsored cohort (processor/export buyer), paid POC, or grant-supported study — participation and funding are separate.
