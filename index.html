    What this does NOT do This tool does not harvest, does not deploy drones, robotic arms, or any extraction equipment, does not assess crew or vessel safety, does not certify seaworthiness, does not grant or verify permits, and does not determine legal harvest rights. The autonomous drone harvester is a future research target, not a current capability. A qualified human makes every go/no-go call.
